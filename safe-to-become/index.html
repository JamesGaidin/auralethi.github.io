Oregon starting points · existing services

Find a living situation that fits your needs

Looking for a place to live, more support where you live now, or help leaving an unsafe situation? Different services handle different needs. Start with the situation closest to yours and contact the provider or referral agency to confirm eligibility and current options.

I need a group home or supported living

For people with intellectual or developmental disabilities, Oregon offers several possible residential arrangements, including 24-hour homes, adult foster homes, and supported living.

  1. Start with your county's developmental-disability program. In Washington County, call 503-846-4737 for intake or eligibility questions.
  2. Ask for a services coordinator and describe what support and living arrangements you would prefer.
  3. Explore the state's residential provider and vacancy information with your coordinator. Ask about licensing, accessibility, personal choice and actual availability.

See Washington County adult I/DD services. A listed vacancy is not a confirmed placement.

I want to stay in my own home with support

Supported living and in-home assistance may help with daily activities while preserving independence.

  1. Contact Washington County developmental-disability case management if I/DD services may apply.
  2. If you need aging, disability or long-term-care guidance, call the Oregon ADRC at 855-673-2372.
  3. Ask what assessments, support plans or funding programs are available and whether you may choose your service provider.
I need emergency shelter or transitional housing

In Washington County, shelter placements commonly begin with a referral rather than arriving unannounced at a residence.

  1. Contact Washington County shelter services or call Community Connect at 503-640-3263.
  2. Explain whether you are unhoused or at imminent risk of losing housing and ask how to enter the coordinated referral process.
  3. Ask about family needs, disability accommodations, waiting lists and alternative options if no placement is available.
I need to leave an abusive or dangerous living situation

Specialist advocates can help discuss confidential shelter and safety options. Publishing individual shelter addresses can create risks, so we link to established referral services instead.

  1. If danger is immediate, call 911.
  2. For confidential Oregon domestic-violence support, call Call to Safety at 888-235-5333.
  3. Ask the advocate for additional local support and shelter referral options. Their guidance can help avoid opening more sensitive pages on a shared device.

Privacy: Someone else may be monitoring your device or browsing activity. Consider a trusted device if you need to look for assistance discreetly.

Quick Exit: Use the fixed button at the lower left, or press Escape twice quickly, to replace this page with the Google homepage. This does not erase browser history, downloads, searches, phone records, or protect against device monitoring. The National Domestic Violence Hotline offers technology-safety guidance, but visiting an external site may create a revealing history entry. Consider using a device the other person cannot access.

Your device · Your choices

Browsing privacy: what you can control

Important: If someone monitors your device, deleting history or changing settings may be visible to them and could increase risk. Consider using a trusted device at a library, workplace, or with someone you trust. You do not have to clean up your device to ask for help. The Quick Exit button remains available at the bottom of this page.

Chrome on Android
  1. Open Chrome, tap the three-dot menu, and choose Delete browsing data.
  2. Choose a time range. Check what will be deleted before continuing; the default may only cover recent activity.
  3. Use More options to review History, Cookies and site data, and Cached images and files. Select only what you intend to clear, then confirm.

Browser history is not the same as Google account search activity. If signed in and syncing, changes may affect other devices.

Safari on iPhone or iPad
  1. Open Settings → Apps → Safari.
  2. Choose Clear History and Website Data.
  3. Review the timeframe and confirm only if that is appropriate for your situation.

Clearing Safari does not erase records kept by websites, other apps, or someone monitoring the device.

Firefox on Android
  1. Open Firefox and tap the three-dot menu.
  2. Choose Settings → Delete browsing data.
  3. Review the categories you want removed, then choose Delete browsing data.

Firefox also lets you remove individual visited pages from its History list. Be aware that changes to history can be noticed.

What clearing browser history cannot do

It cannot reliably remove network logs, monitoring software, shared-account activity, downloaded files, screenshots, notification previews, call or text records, or another person's own records. Private browsing also does not make a monitored device safe.

If a device or account may be monitored, a specialist advocate can help you consider a safer way to access support. Avoid making sudden settings changes if they could put you at greater risk.
