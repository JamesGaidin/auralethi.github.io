## Golden rules
- Human agency and dignity are the mission; technology is a means, not the protagonist.
- Maintain the two organizing concepts: **Free to Meet** and **Safe to Become**.
- Describe work truthfully: **available / in development / research / future vision**; do not market proposed services as operational.
- Work on a feature branch; do not publish to `main` without explicit human approval.
- No AI-generated public news directly from private research or repositories without human disclosure approval.
- Document material decisions, changes, tests, publication state, and next steps in `docs/project/`.
- Preserve existing public routes, notably `/about`, until redirects and links are verified.
- Prefer low-cost static GitHub Pages-compatible solutions until dedicated infrastructure is justified.
- For a new conversation: read these documents, verify against HEAD, report mismatches before acting.
