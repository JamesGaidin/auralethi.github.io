Relational Surface Risk

What happens when interactions with AI accumulate across time? Relational Surface Risk considers effects on trust, dependence, decisions and personal agency beyond a single response.

Status: Working paper and inquiry. This page describes research and development, not an available public service.

The research question

A system can appear acceptable in individual outputs while the longer pattern of interaction may shape people in difficult-to-measure ways.

The framework

This work explores ways to define, investigate and challenge cumulative relational effects. It is an early-stage framework, not an established empirical finding.
