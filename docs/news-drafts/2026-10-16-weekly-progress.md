# Building pathways, not just pages

**Week of October 9–16, 2026**  
**Editorial status: UNPUBLISHED DRAFT. Founder review required before any website release.**

Auralethi took a meaningful step forward this week: our website is becoming easier to explore, and its resource pages are beginning to show visitors where to look for practical help. The goal is simple: make information easier to find while respecting each person's dignity, privacy, and choices.

### Completed in the public website code

The refreshed website now presents Auralethi's mission through **Free to Meet** and **Safe to Become**. A new **Our Journey** timeline records major milestones with links to public evidence. The Research area also offers clearer introductions to ongoing work, including Serenari as an experimental research project, not a public service or a claim of proven autonomy. [Website foundation](https://github.com/JamesGaidin/auralethi.github.io/pull/1) · [Our Journey](https://github.com/JamesGaidin/auralethi.github.io/pull/10) · [Research story](https://github.com/JamesGaidin/auralethi.github.io/pull/12)

Visitors can now explore an initial **Oregon Resource Navigator**, community resource starting points, and guidance for supported living and shelter. The site also gained a **Crisis Help** panel and a **Quick Exit** option, alongside resource pathways for LGBTQ+ youth and families, autistic people, and those supporting them. These are information and referral features, not Auralethi-operated counseling, housing, or case-management services. [Community resources](https://github.com/JamesGaidin/auralethi.github.io/pull/14) · [Resource Navigator](https://github.com/JamesGaidin/auralethi.github.io/pull/15) · [Crisis Help](https://github.com/JamesGaidin/auralethi.github.io/pull/16) · [Living and shelter](https://github.com/JamesGaidin/auralethi.github.io/pull/17) · [Quick Exit and further guidance](https://github.com/JamesGaidin/auralethi.github.io/pull/18)

### In progress

The changes above have been **merged into the public website repository**. That verifies the code changes, not that every feature has passed live-device testing. Accessibility, mobile behavior, external-resource accuracy, and privacy wording still need further review. A Quick Exit can replace a tab's page, but it cannot erase all browsing records or defeat device monitoring.

### Next steps

We'll continue checking referral information and testing how clearly and safely people can navigate these pages. When a first resource isn't suitable, the guidance should make the next option easier to find.

**Free to meet. Safe to become.** We build, test, and improve, one useful step at a time.
