Auralethi research program

Serenari

Serenari is the local implementation effort connected to CortexOS, developing and exercising architectural ideas on Auralethi-controlled hardware.

Status: Implementation and testing. This page describes research and development, not an available public service.

What we're building

The work brings concepts from CortexOS into an implementation that can be inspected, tested, challenged and improved. It is not a housing project.

Why governance matters

The implementation should provide evidence that the rules described in design documents are enforced in operation, not merely promised in prose.

Current boundary

Some milestones and test results are documented internally, but public capability claims will be limited to artifacts approved for publication.
