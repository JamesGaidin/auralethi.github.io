Curiosity is where everything begins.

Some questions become experiments. Some become systems. Others lead somewhere nobody expected. We share our investigations and unanswered questions because ideas grow when more people can examine them.

For the independently curious

Open research backlog

Questions for other people to explore, not a work queue for Auralethi. These are starting points, not validated claims or commissioned tasks.

Research can mean finding a paper that already answers the question. It can mean disproving our assumptions. It can mean building an entirely different solution. All are valuable. These example prompts are proposed for founder review and aren't a claim that an investigation is staffed or funded.
Open research questions are being curated for public release. We maintain a separate, admitted research backlog and will publish entries here only after review under our Suggest → Discuss → Admit → Release process. In the meantime, independent researchers are welcome to explore the programs and publications above.

Interested in sharing a finding? Our public contact channels are still being established. Until then, you're welcome to explore these questions independently. No contribution, affiliation, or response is required.
